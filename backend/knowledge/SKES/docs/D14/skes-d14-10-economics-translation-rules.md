# 10. Economics Translation Rules

## 10.1 K-ETS Carbon Cost Bridge

```text
Gross verified emissions
- free allocation actually received
- banked eligible allowances
- eligible offsets/credits applied
= net allowance requirement
× actual auction/market acquisition price
+ transaction/financing cost
= compliance carbon cash cost
```

유상할당 비율은 곧바로 `배출량×비율×KAU가격`과 동일하지 않다. BM/할당, 발전량, 효율, bank, allocation adjustment가 반영돼야 한다.

## 10.2 48E Project Return Bridge

```text
Gross project CAPEX
→ qualified tax basis
× verified applicable ITC rate
→ gross credit
- transaction/transfer discount
- tax/legal compliance cost
- potential PFE/PWA recapture exposure reserve
= realized tax benefit
```

`30%`는 KCE 모든 BESS의 자동 수령률이 아니다.

## 10.3 Permit Delay NPV

```text
Delay Cost = IDC + EPC escalation + standby/remobilization
           + lost operating margin + lost/changed incentive
           + extension/LD cost + hedge/contract mismatch
```

Quynh Lap은 2031 mechanism cutoff, KCE는 tax placed-in-service/contract milestone, EverCharge는 utility energization delay를 각각 별도 이벤트로 계산한다.

## 10.4 Safeguard Carbon Bridge

```text
Covered emissions
- facility baseline
= exceedance
- valid SMC/ACCU surrendered
= residual compliance position
× actual unit acquisition cost
= compliance cost
```

E&S 경제적 지분귀속은 JV agreement/D13에 따라 별도 처리한다.
