# 13. CCS and Low-Carbon-LNG Supply Chain

## 13.1 Lifecycle Boundary

Honeywell ASCC, KIER·CE TECH 공동연구, Barossa–Darwin–Bayu-Undan CCS는 기술협력·실증·계획 영역이다. 이를 이미 대규모 상용조달이 이루어진 공급망으로 저장하지 않는다.

## 13.2 CCS Procurement Packages

| Package | 공급품/서비스 | 필수 데이터 | 계약상 핵심 |
|---|---|---|---|
| capture technology | solvent·absorber·stripper·IP | capture rate·energy·degradation | license·performance guarantee |
| compression | compressor·dryer·cooler | purity·pressure·availability | LTSA·spares |
| transport | pipeline/ship·meter | quantity·quality·custody | tariff·liability |
| storage | well·injection·monitoring | injectivity·pressure·plume | capacity/right·closure |
| MRV | meters·models·verification | calibration·mass balance | data/audit right |

## 13.3 Low-Carbon LNG Chain-of-Custody

| Stage | 배출/감축 데이터 | 증빙 |
|---|---|---|
| upstream | methane·fuel·flare·vent | meter·method·operator report |
| liquefaction | fuel/electricity·CO₂ | train allocation |
| shipping | fuel·distance·cargo loss | noon report·bunker |
| terminal | power·BOG·sendout | historian·meter |
| power | fuel·generation·heat rate | custody/CEMS |
| capture/storage | captured·transported·injected | MRV plan·verification |

탄소집약도는 cargo/계약/고객별 allocation 방법론을 함께 저장해야 하며, 단순 평균값을 저탄소 LNG claim으로 사용하지 않는다.
