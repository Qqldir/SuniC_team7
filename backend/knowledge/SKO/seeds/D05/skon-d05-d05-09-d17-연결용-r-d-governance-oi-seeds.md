# D05-09. D17 연결용 R&D Governance OI Seeds

```yaml
oi_seeds:

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RND-001
    title: Battery R&D Portfolio Stage-Gate System
    problem:
      - Multiple technology tracks require common continuation and termination criteria
    required_capability:
      - Technology scorecard
      - Pilot-yield gate
      - Customer-validation gate
      - Economic viability gate
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RND-002
    title: Research-to-Patent-to-Product Traceability
    problem:
      - Papers, patents, pilot projects and products are difficult to connect consistently
    required_capability:
      - Knowledge graph
      - Patent family normalization
      - Product evidence linkage
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RND-003
    title: Joint Research IP Contract Standard
    problem:
      - University and technology-company projects can create ambiguous foreground IP
    required_capability:
      - Background IP register
      - Inventorship rules
      - Improvement-IP clause
      - Publication review
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RND-004
    title: AI-Assisted Invention Governance
    problem:
      - AI Researcher output may affect inventorship, confidentiality and patent evidence
    required_capability:
      - Human contribution log
      - Prompt and model-version record
      - Data provenance
      - Invention disclosure workflow
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RND-005
    title: Pilot Plant Digital Research Record
    problem:
      - Material, process, cell and inspection data must remain traceable across pilot experiments
    required_capability:
      - Experiment genealogy
      - Recipe versioning
      - Sample identity
      - Patent-sensitive access control
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RND-006
    title: Independent Technology Validation Network
    problem:
      - Company test results require reproducible third-party validation before customer nomination
    candidate_partners:
      - Universities
      - Government laboratories
      - Certification institutions
      - OEM test centers
    priority: HIGH
```
