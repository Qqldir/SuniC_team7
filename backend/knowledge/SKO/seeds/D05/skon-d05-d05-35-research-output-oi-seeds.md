> SK온 · D05 R&D·특허·지식재산

# D05-35. Research Output OI Seeds

```yaml
oi_seeds:

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RO-001
    title: Paper–Patent–Pilot Traceability Platform
    problem:
      - Research papers, patent families and pilot experiments are disconnected
    required_capability:
      - DOI and patent-family normalization
      - Author–inventor identity resolution
      - Experiment and sample genealogy
      - Product-stage tracking
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RO-002
    title: Solid-State Research Reproducibility Network
    problem:
      - Research-cell results are difficult to compare across universities
    candidate_partners:
      - University of Texas at Austin
      - Hanyang University
      - Seoul National University
      - Yonsei University
      - KICET
    required_output:
      - Common test cell
      - Common pressure and temperature conditions
      - Shared failure-analysis protocol
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RO-003
    title: Publication-to-Scale-Up Review Gate
    problem:
      - Strong paper performance does not guarantee large-area manufacturability
    required_gate:
      - Material reproducibility
      - Electrode-area scale-up
      - Pilot yield
      - Cost model
      - Safety validation
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RO-004
    title: Joint Research Foreground-IP Dashboard
    problem:
      - Joint publications can conceal unclear ownership of follow-up inventions
    required_capability:
      - Background IP declaration
      - Author and inventor separation
      - Joint ownership status
      - Improvement-right tracking
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RO-005
    title: Automated Paper-to-Experiment Research Agent
    problem:
      - Published research results are not automatically converted into reproducible internal experiments
    sk_on_asset:
      - AI Researcher
      - Internal experimental database
    external_capability:
      - Scientific information extraction
      - Protocol generation
      - Unit and condition normalization
      - Evidence citation
    priority: HIGH

  - seed_id: OI-SEED-D05-RO-006
    title: Negative-Result Knowledge Base
    problem:
      - Failed formulations and scale-up routes are rarely visible in papers
    required_capability:
      - Confidential experiment logging
      - Failure taxonomy
      - Similar-experiment retrieval
      - Access control
    priority: HIGH
```
