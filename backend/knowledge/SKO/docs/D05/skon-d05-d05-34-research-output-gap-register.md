> SK온 · D05 R&D·특허·지식재산

# D05-34. Research Output Gap Register

```yaml
research_output_gaps:

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-001
    subject: LLZO paper metadata
    gap:
      - Canonical title
      - DOI
      - Journal
      - Full author list
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-002
    subject: SIPE patent linkage
    gap:
      - No direct patent family confirmed
      - Joint ownership status unknown
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-003
    subject: Surface-modified lithium IP
    gap:
      - Direct Hanyang–SK On patent family not confirmed
      - Scale-up process IP unknown
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-004
    subject: GPE curing IP
    gap:
      - Residual-monomer measurement patent
      - Rapid-curing process patent
      - Commercial process linkage
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-005
    subject: LMRO authorship and IP mapping
    gap:
      - Individual SK On author affiliation requires audit
      - Direct LMRO patent family not confirmed
      - Corporate article link mismatch
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-006
    subject: Ultrahigh-nickel paper-to-patent link
    gap:
      - Direct corresponding patent not confirmed
      - Older NCM composition family is not equivalent
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-007
    subject: Scale-up evidence
    gap:
      - Electrode area
      - Cell capacity
      - Pilot lot size
      - Yield
      - Repeatability across lots
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-008
    subject: Research-to-product conversion
    gap:
      - Which paper-derived technologies entered prototypes
      - Customer sample status
      - Product nomination
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-009
    subject: Negative research results
    gap:
      - Failed formulations
      - Rejected scale-up routes
      - Abandoned research programs
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-010
    subject: Publication and patent timing
    gap:
      - Invention disclosure date
      - Patent filing before publication
      - Publication-review approval
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-011
    subject: Author–inventor identity
    gap:
      - Korean and English name normalization
      - Organization changes
      - ORCID and patent identity mapping
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RO-012
    subject: Digital R&D publications
    gap:
      - AI Researcher model architecture
      - Prediction accuracy
      - Materials AI experimental validation
    priority: VERY_HIGH
```
