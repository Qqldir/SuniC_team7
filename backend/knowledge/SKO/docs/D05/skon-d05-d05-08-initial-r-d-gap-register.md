> SK온 · D05 R&D·특허·지식재산

# D05-08. Initial R&D Gap Register

```yaml
rnd_gaps:

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-001
    subject: Public R&D organization chart
    gap:
      - Division and team-level structure not disclosed
      - Program ownership not disclosed
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-002
    subject: R&D expenditure
    gap:
      - SK On standalone annual R&D expense
      - Program-level budget
      - Capitalized versus expensed R&D
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-003
    subject: Research staffing
    gap:
      - Researcher count
      - Chemistry and engineering skill composition
      - Program-specific staffing
    priority: HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-004
    subject: Pilot plant specification
    gap:
      - Cell capacity and form factor
      - Annual throughput
      - Yield
      - Equipment list
      - Sampling and customer validation
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-005
    subject: Global Quality-Control Center
    gap:
      - Exact completion status
      - Relationship to current quality division
      - Final facility scope
    priority: MEDIUM_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-006
    subject: Program milestones
    gap:
      - Internal stage-gate system
      - Go or stop criteria
      - Prototype and sample classification
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-007
    subject: Patent linkage
    gap:
      - Technology-to-patent family mapping
      - Current legal status
      - Remaining patent life
      - Geographic coverage
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-008
    subject: Joint research IP
    gap:
      - Ownership allocation
      - Exclusive and non-exclusive rights
      - Improvement patent rights
      - Publication-review terms
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-009
    subject: AI Researcher governance
    gap:
      - Training-data provenance
      - Model IP ownership
      - Customer RFQ confidentiality
      - Inventorship for AI-assisted inventions
    priority: VERY_HIGH

  - gap_id: GAP-D05-RND-010
    subject: Research-to-product conversion
    gap:
      - Which research results entered commercial products
      - Customer qualification status
      - Abandoned or paused projects
    priority: VERY_HIGH
```
