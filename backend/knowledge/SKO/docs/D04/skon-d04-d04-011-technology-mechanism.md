### Technology Mechanism

```text
Nickel ratio increase
→ Higher reversible capacity
→ Higher potential energy density
→ Longer driving-range potential

Nickel ratio increase
→ Lower structural and thermal stability
→ Greater interface and gas risk
→ Requires coating, doping, electrolyte and crystal control
```
