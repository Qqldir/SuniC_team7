# 6.5 D17 연결용 Customer Need Map

```yaml
customer_need_map:

  automotive_oem:
    needs:
      - Lower battery cost
      - Faster charging
      - High energy density
      - Multi-form-factor supply
      - Localized production
      - Stable critical-mineral sourcing
      - Reduced warranty risk

  ess_developer:
    needs:
      - Low cost per installed kWh
      - Fire-code compliance
      - Bankable warranty
      - Long cycle life
      - Predictive diagnostics
      - Local production
      - Rapid project delivery

  ai_data_center:
    needs:
      - High availability
      - Fast response
      - Compact footprint
      - Zero-downtime maintenance
      - Fire-risk containment
      - Power-quality stabilization

  robot_oem:
    needs:
      - High cell-level energy density
      - Compact battery design
      - Opportunity charging
      - Safe indoor operation
      - Fleet-level battery optimization

  used_ev_platform:
    needs:
      - Fast battery diagnosis
      - Objective SOH standard
      - Residual-value certification
      - Fraud-resistant history data
      - Consumer-trust mechanism

  fleet_operator:
    needs:
      - Remaining-life prediction
      - Charging-cost optimization
      - Preventive maintenance
      - Vehicle uptime
      - Battery replacement timing
```
