> SK온 · D07 생산거점·캐파

# D07-06. Plant Status Timeline

```yaml
plant_timeline:

  2018:
    - Seosan existing expansion operation

  2020:
    - Komarom 1 commercial operation
    - Changzhou JV commercial operation

  2021:
    - Yancheng 1 operation
    - Huizhou JV operation

  2022:
    - Komarom 2 operation
    - Georgia 1 operation
    - Georgia 2 operation
    - Yancheng 2 operation

  2024:
    - Ivancsa partial operation

  2025:
    - Kentucky 1 partial operation
    - Yancheng 3 partial operation
    - BlueOval SK restructuring agreement

  2026:
    - BlueOval SK transaction closes on May 20
    - Kentucky plants transferred to Ford
    - SK On Tennessee standalone entity established
    - HSBMA commercial production starts on June 1
    - China JV stake-swap correction filed on June 22

  2028_target:
    - SK On Tennessee mass-production start
```

이 Timeline은 상업가동·부분가동·소유권 이전을 구분한다. 특히 `공장 준공`, `부분가동`, `상업생산 개시`, `설계 Capacity 도달`은 서로 다른 Event로 저장해야 한다. ([KIND][1])
